# Recovery boundaries

The public candidate is a clean source subset, not a backup of every private development asset. A successful GitHub push cannot justify deleting local research.

Local preservation has three distinct layers:
1. Complete private Git/ref and dirty-source snapshots.
2. Private generated/runtime and selected gameplay-evidence archives, with per-member SHA-256 readback.
3. A no-follow file/identity/allocation inventory and a list of remaining unique or externally referenced materials.

The owner's detailed local archive lists original locations and hashes; those private paths and reports are not included here. Same-volume copies do not protect against drive failure. A verified copy on another physical device is needed before deleting unique material.

Generated C may require exact analysis inputs, metadata, generator revision, options and original game input. Build caches are reproducible only if these remain available. PDBs may be the only matching symbols for a historical EXE. Shared/hardlinked resources and historical recovery references must be checked before removal.

Do not delete on filename, age or matching length. Do not automatically apply cleanup suggestions. Recheck hashes, file identities, active dependencies and the separately approved deletion list immediately before an authorized cleanup.

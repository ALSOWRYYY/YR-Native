# Contributions

Active maintenance is suspended. Do not interpret a roadmap item as a request to restart development.

Contributions should be small and identify their origin and license. Include the applicable file list, behavior tested and evidence limitations. Keep generated commercial expressions, binaries, assets, vendor DLLs, private mods, credentials, screenshots, dumps and personal reports out of patches.

Prefer authored fixtures and public specifications. Do not claim game behavior from parser tests, DLL loading, lookup timing or command acknowledgments. Do not weaken safety checks to make a test pass.

Never silently broaden the license of inherited or third-party code. Any additional engine source requires review before inclusion. Do not send sensitive logs in public issues; first produce a minimal sanitized description.

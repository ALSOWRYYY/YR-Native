| Project and fixed scope | License evidence | Archive decision |

| redalert2-recomp independently authored host/tooling | MIT with separate game-derived exclusion in NOTICE | Only the reviewed generic storage tools and tests are included, with original notices |

| Phobos dd03ca7e306787f05a90f1f69b51a1b41b71fffe | LGPLv3 | No source or DLL included |
